# Companion Journal: changes

## 1.43.1 (2026-11-13)

- The book opens faster; told books are kept in memory
- Same text, word for word

## 1.43.0 (2026-11-13)

- Levels at their minute; killers named only for their own victim
- A first seen only in passing is not a moment or a title
- Chapter titles come from the story, not quest names
- Each chapter ends where the night really ended
- Companion books: counts and "we" checked against the companion's own record

## 1.42.0 (2026-11-13)

- A level is told after what came just before it
- Companion books: a bad stretch of deaths in one sentence, with its cause

## 1.41.0 (2026-11-13)

- "Us" in a death only when a companion fell then too
- Places only as precise as the record
- Companion books: every death of the companion counted right

## 1.40.0 (2026-11-12)

- Every line sits at the minute it happened
- Class quests stay "I" even in a group
- Each chapter starts where the night really began

## 1.39.0 (2026-11-12)

- Chapters end on the outcome of their goal
- Travel lines only from what you really did, each once
- "New to me" unless the book knows it was new to both

## 1.38.0 (2026-11-12)

- Companions join and leave at their real times
- While grouped, hearthstones and flights are "we"
- Companion books: a friend is named a friend once, threads closed

## 1.37.0 (2026-11-12)

- Each night ends on its goal, the late road folded into the close
- A companion leaving and coming back is told
- Open threads are closed or said to wait
- Companion books keep the step a later scene needs

## 1.36.0 (2026-11-12)

- Steps a later chapter depends on are kept
- Trips with no quest are told as what they were
- Companion books: threads closed, her falls in the worst stretches told

## 1.35.0 (2026-11-12)

- Each night is told to its true end
- Deaths and rescues in the order they happened
- Companion books: "we" only while together, late joins told as joins

## 1.34.0 (2026-11-12)

- Chapters close on where you ended, with whom, and what is left open
- Shorter paragraphs; hand-ins told as handing over
- Companion books: long chapters keep what you two did together

## 1.33.0 (2026-11-12)

- Deaths in one place are one sentence about the danger
- An item is told where it came from the first time
- Companion books: real quest stories, the whole party, record order

## 1.32.0 (2026-11-12)

- One adventure per paragraph
- Deaths are worded differently each time
- Every new land is reached, never jumped to
- Companion books: no quest titles, "we" only while grouped

## 1.31.0 (2026-11-12)

- Journeys are told leg by leg, before where they led
- Kills are told with the reason we went
- Companion books follow your own book's adventures while they were there

## 1.30.0 (2026-11-12)

- Companions are named from the first night they were with you
- A trail of quests is told as one goal
- Companion books: the road back as you walked it, partings where they happened

## 1.29.0 (2026-11-12)

- Everyone is introduced once, when they first appear
- Threads the book opens are closed
- Journeys follow the road you really took, zeppelin or on foot
- Companion books: people in their real places, a friend's own falls told

## 1.28.0 (2026-11-12)

- Named kills and story-moving quests are kept
- Several deaths in one place are one sentence about that place
- Companions appear when they joined, introduced once
- An object found once is told once
- Companion books: her levels and deeds beside yours, in words of her own
- Two companions' books never share a sentence

## 1.27.0 (2026-11-12)

- Records everyone in your group, and how long they stayed
- Records elite fights in the open world
- Short groups with strangers are told as small adventures
- Long walks through many lands are told as journeys
- Danger changes the words, from our level against the land's
- Companion books: "we", the bond, and shared firsts lead each chapter
- A quest is told in the place and order it happened

## 1.26.0 (2026-11-11)

- A bad stretch of deaths is told in a few sentences, in order
- A killer met again is named once ("the Rot Hides")
- People are introduced when they arrive, before anything they do
- Levels and counts go to the chapter's close, not mid-paragraph
- Bosses get the right pronoun, or none when unknown
- A run never says a boss died when it did not
- "Dahlia and Grosh were", not "was"

## 1.25.0 (2026-11-11)

- Deaths, levels and rare sightings join the sentence where they happened
- A dungeon run keeps its bosses as one progression, losses in one sentence
- Zones you only cross are named, not described
- Lifts, bats and zeppelins appear only when they lead somewhere
- A chapter closes where it began
- Items and people a later chapter needs are kept
- Meeting someone the book already named earns a feeling again
- Campfire people: shorter tooltip; README on one screen, history in docs

## 1.24.0 (2026-11-11)

- The Current book picks which quests to tell, like the Planned storyteller.
- No more bare acts chained with "then" and "also".
- Each quest gets its own account, one line, or nothing.
- A setup stays with its payoff; what leads nowhere goes first.
- Switch it off: Settings > Testing, book variant `select`.

## 1.23.0 (2026-11-11)

**Changed**
- The evening is told as episodes, one paragraph each, in order.
- A dungeon run is one adventure: who went, why, the wipes, the bosses.
- A stretch of deaths is one passage; the count said once.
- A quick zone crossing and back is not told; lifts and flights make one journey.
- A companion is never "a stranger"; "we" only when someone was with you.
- The level is said once a chapter, where it came.
- Campfire people keep no journal and leave your list two hours after the fire.
- Restart the game once after updating; `/reload` is not enough.

## 1.22.0 (2026-11-11)

- A fresh install starts with the author's settings; existing installs keep theirs.
- People: display names, nameplate icons, tooltips, last seen, menu and campfire start on.
- Book: large size, two pages, style B, story voice and the new-chapter notice start on.

## 1.21.0 (2026-11-10)

Planned storyteller (Settings > Testing), still off by default:

**Changed**
- A planned chapter of your own book holds 340 words at most.
- Each deed is told in full, in one line, or not at all; two bare acts at most.
- Work is told in the order it happened; a quest chain stays in one paragraph.
- People, places and things are introduced once in full, then by short name.
- The last level of the night closes the chapter.
- A feeling always ends its paragraph.
- Companion chapters: "died again" only after an earlier death; one opener a chapter.
- Restart the game once after updating.
